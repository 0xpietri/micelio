# Priors are beliefs you can update

A prior is where you start, not where you must stay. Evidence moves it through [[Bayes rule]].

> [!said]
> So the prior is just my first guess, and every new fact nudges it.

A strong prior needs a lot of evidence to move; a weak one follows the data quickly.
