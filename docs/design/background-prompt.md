Wide landscape illustration, 1536 x 1024 (3:2), for the background of a website. It is ONLY a backdrop: other artwork (mushrooms, white mycelium threads, glowing spore clouds and text) will be placed on top of it later in code, so leave room for them.

STYLE
1960s–70s psychedelic concert-poster art meets modern flat vector illustration. Bold, uniform black outlines (about 4 px at this size) around every shape, flat color fills with smooth gradients, no photorealism, no 3D render look, no grain or noise. Saturated neon palette, exactly these colors: hot magenta #FF2FA0, neon orange #FF8A1F, sun yellow #FFD23F, acid lime #C6FF3A, neon green #39FF6A, electric cyan #22D3EE, electric blue #3B5BFF, ultraviolet #9B5CFF, outline black-violet #12051F. Complementary colors placed side by side so they vibrate (magenta next to green, orange next to blue).

COMPOSITION (positions as % of width x height)
- Horizon / soil line: a slightly wavy horizontal line at 48 % of the height, running edge to edge. Above it is the sky world, below it the underground world.
- Sky (top 48 %): liquid, melting horizontal bands of color that ripple and swirl like oil on water, flowing gently left to right. An op-art sun made of thick concentric rings (yellow, magenta, cyan, cream, a thin black ring between each) centered at 78 % across and 20 % down, about 30 % of the image height in diameter, its rings slightly warped by the same liquid ripple.
- Keep the top-left area (0–50 % across, 0–20 % down) calmer and a bit darker so white and yellow title text stays readable there.
- Two layers of distant mountains right above the horizon: the back layer jagged, magenta fading to ultraviolet; the front layer rounder, cyan fading to deep indigo. Both with black outlines, peaks no higher than 30 % down from the top.
- A thin strip of bright green grassy hills sitting exactly on the horizon line, between 44 % and 48 % of the height, with tiny cartoon flowers (yellow and magenta dots) here and there.
- Underground (bottom 52 %): a stained-glass mosaic of irregular polygon cells separated by thick black lead lines, like a cathedral window seen from inside the earth. Cell fills are deep, dark jewel tones (dark ultraviolet, deep magenta, midnight blue, dark teal) with a subtle marbled, oily swirl inside each cell. It gets darker toward the bottom edge. Keep this layer LOW contrast and dark overall, so thin white glowing lines and white dotted spore clouds drawn on top later stand out clearly.
- A few small rounded stones embedded in the mosaic near the bottom (one yellow, one cyan, one orange), outlined in black.

DO NOT INCLUDE
No mushrooms or fungi of any kind, no trees, no roots, no white lines or threads underground, no people, no animals, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no frame or border. Nothing large standing on the horizon line: the mushrooms will be placed there.

MOOD
Joyful, dreamy, alive and slightly trippy, like the ground is breathing. Clean enough to sit behind a user interface.
